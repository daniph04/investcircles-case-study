# AI-assisted development disclosure

InvestCircles was developed with substantial assistance from AI coding tools.

Daniel Peña Hartwig originated the concept and directed the product work: defining the problem, product rules, user flows, data requirements, privacy boundaries, constraints, and acceptance criteria. AI tools were used across implementation, debugging, test generation, review, and documentation. Their output was iterated and evaluated against application behavior, automated checks, and explicit evidence rules.

This repository does not claim that every line of the private product source was written manually. It also does not treat AI-generated output as proof of correctness. The evidence is the combination of product decisions, working software, dated tests and builds, observed failures, and clearly documented limitations.

No model provider is presented as an employer, partner, sponsor, or endorser of the project.
